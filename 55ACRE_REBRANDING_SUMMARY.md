## 🎨 Color Usage Guide

### Black (#000000)
- **Used for**: Hero section background, buttons (primary), text headings, borders, navbar
- **Effect**: Creates strong contrast, premium feel

### Dark Gray (#1F2937 - 800)
- **Used for**: Hero gradient, button hover states, secondary backgrounds
- **Effect**: Softer than pure black, elegant

### Light Gray (#F3F4F6 - 100)
- **Used for**: Section backgrounds, card backgrounds, light accents
- **Effect**: Creates breathing room, reduces contrast fatigue

### White (#FFFFFF)
- **Used for**: Text on dark backgrounds, primary buttons, borders, footer icons
- **Effect**: Maximum contrast, emphasis

### Gray Text (#D1D5DB - 300)
- **Used for**: Secondary text, descriptions, footer text
- **Effect**: Readable yet subtle
